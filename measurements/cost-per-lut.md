tile -- the full routed outline, so each LUT4 carries its share of the

## Cost per LUT4 (estimate)

| quantity | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| slot price | USD 8,000 for 1,000 dies | wafer.space GF180MCU Run 3, standard 1×1 slot ([wafer.space/price](https://wafer.space/price)), retrieved 2026-11-19 |
| usable area per die | 12.92 mm² inside the default pad ring | same listing |

Arithmetic: USD 8,000 ÷ 1,000 dies = USD 8.00 per die;
USD 8.00 ÷ 12.92 mm² = USD 0.619195 per mm² of core; one LUT4 occupies 0.0033422 mm² of that core; 0.619195 × 0.0033422 = USD 0.00206947, reported as **USD 0.00207 per LUT4 per die**. Using the DEF-derived
area instead of the record's rounded value lands on the same USD 0.00207 at five decimal places.

**This is an area-proportional shuttle estimate, and it assumes the
whole slot core is available for tiles.** Whole-tile packing, chip-level
logic and I/O, yield, testing and packaging all change the cost of a
*usable* LUT, and the listed price is date-stamped and can change. No
timing, power or yield claim is made or implied here.
