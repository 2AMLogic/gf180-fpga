# Roadmap

PDK and records, item by item, what the open flow can and cannot prove about it.

**Status as of 2026-11-13, `main` @ `08891f4`.** The graded state is
[`manifests/gf180-fpga.signoff.json`](manifests/gf180-fpga.signoff.json);
where this file and that record disagree, the record wins.

## The constraint that shapes everything below

Place-and-route, synthesis, DRC, STA and `manifests/run-signoff.sh` in write
mode all need the pinned gf180mcu PDK (`flow/setup-pdk.sh`) and OpenROAD on the
host. CI has neither and runs only `--check`. The one Builder attempt on #55
stopped because the PDK download timed out on the worker. Until a host with
the PDK takes these, the items that move the T1 count cannot start. `README.md`
and the two workflow files are also pinned by hash in the item 10 inventory, so
editing them requires a write-mode re-attest, which needs the PDK too.

## Not planned

  chain before the tile is verified (`CLAUDE.md`).

  decision record.

  defects found there are filed on `sky130-fpga`.
